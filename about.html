About NexusPay

Building the Financial Infrastructure of Tomorrow

We started with a simple belief: moving money should be as seamless, secure, and instant as sending a message. That vision drives everything we build.

Our Mission

Democratizing Global Finance

NexusPay exists to eliminate the barriers, delays, and excessive fees that have defined international payments for decades. We believe every individual and business — regardless of geography — deserves access to fast, fair, and transparent financial infrastructure.

By combining blockchain technology, artificial intelligence, and modern distributed systems, we have built a payment platform that processes over 2.5 million transactions daily across 180+ countries — with settlement times measured in seconds, not days.

Our Journey

Key Milestones

From a founding team of four to a global fintech platform serving millions.

2019

Company Founded

NexusPay was founded in San Francisco with a $4M seed round. The founding team of four began building the core payment processing engine.

2020

Series A & Beta Launch

Raised $28M Series A. Launched beta platform supporting 15 currencies and 30 countries. Onboarded first 10,000 users within three months.

2021

AI Fraud Engine & Series B

Deployed proprietary AI fraud detection system. Raised $85M Series B. Expanded to 80+ countries and achieved $1B in annual payment volume.

2022

Blockchain Settlement Layer

Launched blockchain-verified settlement infrastructure. Achieved PCI DSS Level 1 and SOC 2 Type II certifications. Team grew to 300+ employees globally.

2023

Enterprise Platform

Released enterprise API platform and developer tools. Secured partnerships with major e-commerce platforms. Processed our 500 millionth transaction.

2024

Series C & Global Expansion

Raised $220M Series C at $2.1B valuation. Expanded to 180+ countries with 150+ currencies. Opened offices in London, Singapore, and São Paulo.

2025

Reaching New Heights

Surpassed 850,000 active users and 2.5M daily transactions. Launched next-generation fraud detection with 99.7% accuracy. Named to Forbes Fintech 50.
